Sweet Rescue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. Kawi Software AS, Norway, makes Sweet Rescue: Match 3 Puzzle, a match 3 game with a bakery street to restore.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. The app creates an anonymous player id so the Weekly League, the Daily Bake and your purchases work.
  • There is no chat, no ads and no selling of data. Player names come from a preset list.
  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we only measure if you agree. Elsewhere measurement is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings. Players below the age threshold for their country are never measured.
  • You can delete everything linked to your player id from Settings at any time (Delete my data), or ask us by email.

What we store

On your device: your game progress, settings, the age band you picked at first launch (birth month and year are not stored, only the band) and your consent choices. On our servers (Supabase, EU region): the anonymous player id, preset display name, level reached, time zone offset, Weekly League pool and points, Daily Bake results (moves, stars, the moves you played) and a record of purchases (product, time and store transaction id, never payment details).

Product analytics

When analytics is on, we use PostHog (EU servers) to see which parts of the game work: events such as "level won" or "shop opened", screens visited, crash reports and session recordings of the app's own screens with text inputs masked. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland analytics starts only after you tap Accept on the consent card; elsewhere it is on by default. Turn it off any time in Settings > Privacy. Players under the consent age for their country (16 in most of the EU, 13 elsewhere) are never measured.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and by RevenueCat. We receive the product, the purchase status and an anonymous customer id, never your card, Apple ID or Google account details. Coin packs are consumable and never expire. The Welcome Pack can be bought once per player.

Notifications

With your permission the app schedules reminders on your device (lives full, the Daily Bake, league results, new levels), at most one a day and never between 22:00 and 09:00. Nothing is sent from a server. Turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Children

Sweet Rescue is made for players aged 13 and up and is not directed at children. The first-launch age band limits purchases: players under 13 cannot buy anything, players aged 13 to 17 see a smaller shop with a monthly cap. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and younger players are never measured.

Processors

Supabase (database and anonymous sign-in, EU), PostHog (product analytics, EU), RevenueCat (purchase records), Apple and Google (payments). Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

Use Settings > Account > Delete my data in the app, or email support@kawisoftware.com to access, correct or delete your data. We answer within 30 days.
